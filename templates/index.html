How to use this player
  • Play a songDouble-click it, or hover its number and press the play button. On a touch screen, just tap it.
  • More optionsPress the "⋯" button on a song, or right-click it: play next, add to queue, favorite, go to the artist or album.
  • Find musicPick a category in the left sidebar, or use the search box (press /).
  • KeyboardSpace play or pause · ← → skip 10 seconds · N next · P previous · Esc close menus
  • Another device is playing?The bottom bar says "Listening on…" and its buttons control that device. Use the devices button to move playback here.
  • Shuffle and repeat in your desktop media widget (KDE, GNOME)Browsers can only give your desktop play, pause, next, previous and seek. For a real "DeCloud" player with working shuffle, repeat, volume and cover art, run this once on that computer: curl -fsSL /static/tools/decloud-mpris.py -o ~/decloud-mpris.py && python3 ~/decloud-mpris.py Then add --install-service to start it at login.
  • The coloured dotsRed goes back to the DeCloud home screen, yellow hides the sidebar, green goes full screen. Drag the sidebar's edge to resize it.

⚠️ Unrestricted access

Full access lets the voice agent run any shell command on this machine. It can:

  • Delete files and folders (rm, shred)
  • Format or wipe disks (mkfs, dd)
  • Change permissions and users (chmod, chown)
  • Install or remove software
  • Shut down or reboot the machine
  • Read and exfiltrate any file it can access

This could permanently break your setup or expose your data.

Welcome to DeCloud

DeCloud is your personal cloud. A self-hosted dashboard for audiobooks, AI chat, files, music, and more. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud fees. Just your machine, your data, your control.

Your voice assistant will answer to this name. Change it anytime in Settings → Voice.

What's Inside

📚 Audiobooks: Read and listen with real-time word highlighting

🤖 AI Chat: Local LLM via Ollama, or cloud models

📁 Files: Browse your filesystem from your phone

🎨 Generate: AI image generation via ComfyUI

🎵 Music: Your local music library, anywhere

📊 System Monitor: CPU, RAM, GPU in real time

All features are optional. The app works with zero configuration.

Secure by Default

DeCloud binds to localhost only: zero exposed ports. Remote access is through a tunnel (Tailscale Funnel, localhost.run, or Cloudflare), so your machine is never directly reachable.

All API endpoints require a PIN. No accounts, no passwords to leak.
