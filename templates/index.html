Welcome to DeCloud

DeCloud is your personal cloud. It runs on your own computer and lets you reach your books, files, music and AI helpers from your phone or any browser. No accounts, no tracking, no monthly fees.

You'll see these on the My Lab screen. To change them later, replay this walkthrough from Settings → About.

What's Inside

Books — Read, or have your books read aloud, with each word highlighted

AI Chat — Chat with an AI that runs on your own computer

Files — See the files on your computer from your phone

Music — Play your own music collection anywhere

My Lab — See how busy your computer is right now

Everything works out of the box. Extras can be turned on later in Settings.

Your Passcode Is the Key

The passcode you just typed is the only way in. Write it down somewhere safe — a password manager, or paper you keep at home.

Forgot it? On this computer, open a terminal in the DeCloud folder and run ./decloud pin to see it, or ./decloud pin reset for a new one.

Access from Your Phone

Open DeCloud on your phone, then choose "Add to Home Screen" so it works like a regular app.

You're All Set

Tap any app on the home screen to get started. Everything is configurable in Settings.

DeCloud is still young. Found a problem or have an idea? Tell us on GitHub (Settings → About).
